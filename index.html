Outlook Email Assistant

Outlook Email Assistant is a human-in-the-loop email management assistant for Outlook and Live.com mailboxes.

What it does

  • Reviews and classifies Outlook and Live.com messages.
  • Helps identify spam, promotions, newsletters, and other cleanup candidates.
  • Helps preserve receipts, account notices, personal correspondence, and important records.
  • Requires explicit user approval before moving messages to Deleted Items.
  • Can verify that approved messages were moved successfully.

Safety

Outlook Email Assistant does not permanently delete messages, empty Deleted Items, or send email. Mailbox-changing actions require explicit user approval.

Authentication

Access to a mailbox requires user authorization through secure authentication. The assistant accesses mailbox information only through authorized connections.
