Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Outlook Email Assistant is a human-in-the-loop assistant for reviewing and organizing a user's authorized Outlook or Live.com mailbox.

Information accessed

When a user connects a Microsoft account, Outlook Email Assistant may access mailbox information necessary to provide requested features, including message identifiers, subjects, senders, received dates, read status, and message previews.

How information is used

Mailbox information is used to perform user-requested functions such as reviewing messages, identifying cleanup candidates, retrieving specific messages, and verifying mailbox organization actions.

Mailbox actions

Outlook Email Assistant requires explicit user approval before moving a message to Deleted Items. The assistant does not permanently delete messages, empty Deleted Items, or send email.

Authentication and third-party services

Authentication is handled through Auth0 and Microsoft authorization services. Authorized mailbox operations are performed through Microsoft services. These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Credentials

Users should not provide Microsoft passwords directly to Outlook Email Assistant. Account authorization occurs through the applicable secure authentication and authorization flows.

User control

Users control whether to connect their Microsoft account and whether to approve mailbox-changing actions. Users may revoke connected-account authorization through the applicable account or authorization settings.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the service or its data-handling practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
