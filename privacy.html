Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Outlook Email Assistant is a human-in-the-loop assistant for reviewing and organizing a user's authorized Outlook or Live.com mailbox. This policy explains what information may be accessed and how it is used when the service is connected to a Microsoft account.

Information accessed

When a user authorizes a Microsoft account, Outlook Email Assistant may access mailbox information necessary to provide requested features. Depending on the requested operation, this may include Microsoft account profile information and email information such as message identifiers, subjects, senders, received dates, read status, message previews, and message content needed to retrieve or evaluate a selected message.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide user-requested functionality, such as confirming the connected Microsoft account, reviewing messages, identifying cleanup candidates, retrieving specific messages, organizing mailbox information, moving explicitly approved messages to Deleted Items, and verifying those moves.

Mailbox-changing actions

Outlook Email Assistant requires explicit user approval before moving a message to Deleted Items. The assistant does not permanently delete messages, empty Deleted Items, or send email.

Authentication and authorization

Users should not provide Microsoft passwords directly to Outlook Email Assistant. Authentication and authorization are performed through secure OAuth-based authorization flows using Auth0 and Microsoft services. Access is limited to permissions granted through those authorization mechanisms.

Service providers and data recipients

Outlook Email Assistant relies on service providers necessary to operate the service. These may include Microsoft for account authorization and mailbox operations, Auth0 for authentication and authorization, Render for hosting the MCP service, and OpenAI for the ChatGPT/plugin experience through which users may interact with the service.

Information may be transmitted to these providers only as necessary to authenticate users, process user-requested operations, operate the service, maintain security, and return requested results. Those providers may process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Sale and advertising

Outlook Email Assistant does not sell mailbox information or use mailbox information for targeted advertising.

Data retention

Outlook Email Assistant is designed to access mailbox information as needed to perform user-requested operations rather than to create an independent permanent archive of mailbox contents. Service providers may temporarily process or retain information, authentication records, security records, or operational logs as necessary to provide, secure, and troubleshoot their services and in accordance with their applicable policies.

Authorization and deletion controls

Users may revoke connected-account authorization through applicable Microsoft, Auth0, OpenAI, or connected-service controls. Revoking authorization prevents future authorized mailbox access through that connection.

Messages moved to Deleted Items remain subject to the user's Microsoft mailbox controls and retention settings. Outlook Email Assistant does not permanently erase those messages or empty the Deleted Items folder.

Security

Outlook Email Assistant uses authenticated connections and HTTPS-based communications when interacting with supported services. Users should never provide passwords, access tokens, refresh tokens, API secrets, recovery codes, or other authentication secrets directly in chat.

User control

Users control whether to connect a Microsoft account and whether to approve mailbox-changing actions. Users should review proposed cleanup actions before approving them.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the service, its providers, or its data-handling practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
